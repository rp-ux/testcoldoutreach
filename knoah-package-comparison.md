## Media Budget: How It Works

The media budget line (Growth: $10K/mo · Full Scale: $26K/mo) is **client-owned spend** paid directly to Meta, Google, and X ad platforms. ICODA sets up, manages, and optimizes the campaigns — you see every dollar in your ad account.

This is separated from the service fee intentionally: it keeps ICODA's pricing transparent and gives you full visibility and control over your ad spend at all times.
