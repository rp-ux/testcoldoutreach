# Discovery Questions: Matthew Reynolds — The Vegas Walk Method® (iGaming)

Lead asked ICODA to recommend the service ("Let ICODA Experts Decide"), so the discovery
call has to do the qualifying work a self-selected service request normally would. Budget
is tight (<$5,000) and timeline is ASAP, so keep the call focused — these are the questions
that actually change the recommendation, not a generic intake form.

## 1. The offer itself
- What exactly is The Vegas Walk Method® — a casino/betting product, an affiliate site, a
  content brand, or something else? What's the core user action (sign-up, deposit, download)?
- Is there a live product/site today, or is this pre-launch?
- What's the current user base size, if any?

## 2. Regulatory footprint (critical for iGaming)
- Which specific European countries/markets are you targeting first?
- Do you hold licenses for those markets (MGA, UKGC, Curaçao, etc.), and are they in place
  today or pending?
- Any markets or channels we need to explicitly avoid for compliance reasons?

## 3. Audience & positioning
- Who's the target player — casual vs. high-value, age range, language(s)?
- What differentiates The Vegas Walk Method® from competitors already active in these markets?
- Any existing brand assets (creative, landing pages, brand guidelines) we'd build on?

## 4. Channel history
- What's been tried already for user acquisition or community building, and what worked or
  didn't?
- Any existing affiliate, influencer, or paid social relationships in iGaming we'd inherit
  or need to avoid conflicting with?
- Any current Telegram/Discord community, or starting from zero?

## 5. Budget & scope reality check
- Is the <$5,000 a one-time test budget or a recurring monthly budget?
- Does that figure include ad spend, or is it agency/production fees only (ad spend separate)?
- Is there a plan to scale budget if the initial push performs well?

## 6. Timeline & success criteria
- What does "ASAP" mean concretely — a launch date, an event, an investor milestone?
- What would make this engagement a clear win in the first 30/60 days — signups, deposits,
  community size, something else?
- Who signs off on the plan, and is that person on this call?

## 7. Logistics
- Confirm best channel going forward: WhatsApp or Telegram, and what handle/number?
- Any reporting format or cadence you expect (weekly update, dashboard, etc.)?
