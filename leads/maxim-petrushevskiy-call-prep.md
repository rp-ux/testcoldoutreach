## What this means going in

**Pre-launch stage** — there's no site to send paid traffic to yet. Phase 1 has to be foundation work (SEO, content, brand/asset prep, affiliate groundwork), not performance marketing. This also means there's runway to build organic assets before launch, which is the highest-leverage time to start SEO.

**Anjouan license** — cheapest/fastest offshore license tier, but it carries real limits worth surfacing (tactfully) on the call:
- Not accepted by Google/Meta ads policies for gambling — paid search/social will be largely closed regardless of GEO. Channel mix needs to lean SEO, affiliates, influencers, community (Telegram), and native/programmatic.
- Weaker standing with payment processors/banks than Curaçao or MGA — worth asking what payment stack they're planning per GEO, especially for India (UPI/local rails) and RU (crypto is often the practical fallback).
- If they later want compliant paid channels, a license upgrade path (Curaçao) may become a phase 2 conversation — don't push it now, just flag it as a future lever.

**GEO priority — RU-speaking + India top, Turkey B-tier**
- **RU-speaking GEOs**: large, mature, saturated affiliate market; online gambling is restricted domestically in Russia itself, so this is a grey-market play — mirror-domain strategy, Telegram community/channel presence, and Russian-language SEO content matter more than compliant paid.
- **India**: fast-growing, but a regulatory patchwork (legal in only a couple of states; most offshore operators serve Indian players in a grey-market capacity). Needs Hindi/regional-language content, INR pricing, UPI-friendly payment messaging, and cricket-adjacent influencer/affiliate angles.
- **Turkey**: domestically blocked (ISP/BTK blocking of gambling sites is standard), ad platforms prohibited for the vertical — this is the hardest and most resource-intensive GEO per dollar. Makes sense as B-tier / phase 2, not phase 1.

**Budget ($40–50k, phase 1)** — realistic for a focused build in 1–2 priority GEOs, not enough to run full SEO + content + affiliate + localization across all three markets at once. Recommend proposing RU-speaking + India as the phase 1 scope, with Turkey explicitly deferred to phase 2 once the product is live and budget scales — rather than spreading thin across all three now.
